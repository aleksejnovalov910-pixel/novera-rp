# NOVERA RP feature roadmap

## Playable first release
- Stable account login/registration
- Three character slots
- Stable character creation with age instead of date of birth
- MySQL character persistence
- Spawn/reconnect
- HUD and notifications
- Cash/bank economy
- Inventory and item use
- Interaction system
- Starter tutorial
- Starter jobs and salary loop
- Vehicle ownership, keys, garage, fuel and basic dealership
- Property/home ownership baseline
- Phone, tablet and F2 settings/keybind entry points
- Organizations and families baseline
- V-Market baseline
- Admin/logging essentials

## Expansion after baseline
- Full clothing stores and advanced appearance editor
- Real-world-inspired lore vehicle catalog
- VIN, registration, key transfer, lockpicks, GPS tracker, impound, inspection, component damage, insurance repair, oil/battery, DMV plates, vehicle ownership history
- Advanced houses/interiors/furniture
- Government and criminal organizations
- Licenses and weapons systems
- Business economy
- Seasonal pass
- Expanded onboarding/tutorial
- Full V-Market economy and listings

Rule: ship a coherent playable loop before visual perfection. Fix blockers immediately; polish non-blocking UI after the baseline works.
